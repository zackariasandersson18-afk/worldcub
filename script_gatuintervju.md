# Script: Gatuintervju – "I identify as a millionaire"

**Referens:** collaget med de 4 bilderna (ansiktsreferens) → tjejen **till höger** i bild.
**Format:** 9:16, ca 10 s, mobilfilmad halvbild på kvällsgata, gula ord-för-ord-undertexter.
**Modeller:** bilder med Seedream 5.5, video med Wan 3.0.

---

## 1. Bildprompt – startbild (Seedream 5.5, referens: collaget)

```
Use the woman from the reference image (4-photo collage) as the exact same person: same face,
light icy-blue eyes with realistic white sclera, long dark boho knotless braids with curly ends,
warm light brown skin, full glossy lips, small gold hoop earrings. She has a curvy voluptuous
hourglass figure with a full bust, slim waist, wide hips and thick thighs.

Scene: a street interview at night on a city sidewalk, parked cars, warm street lamps and tall
apartment buildings softly blurred in the background, slight mist. Vertical 9:16 iPhone video
frame, medium shot from mid-thigh up, eye level, natural handheld look.

On the RIGHT side of the frame stands the woman (the interviewer), turned toward the man, holding
a handheld microphone with a small white mic flag with a simple red logo. She wears a fitted black
crop top, high-waisted light blue jeans and a thin gold necklace. Calm, curious expression.

On the LEFT side of the frame stands a confident man in his late 20s: slicked-back hair,
sunglasses at night, a shiny oversized gold-colored suit that is clearly too big for him, a fake
chunky gold chain, holding up a car key with a sports-car keychain, smug proud expression,
talking toward the microphone.

Photorealistic, natural skin texture, candid social media look, no text, no watermark.
```

## 2. Bildprompt – slutbild (Seedream 5.5, referens: startbilden + collaget)

```
The first image is the start frame. Same scene, same two people, same clothes, same camera angle
and framing. The woman on the right has turned her head and looks at the man's outfit from head
to toe with a slightly raised eyebrow and a small amused smile, microphone still in hand. The man
on the left looks confused, mouth slightly open, sunglasses slipping down his nose.
Same icy-blue eyes, same face as the collage. Photorealistic, no text.
```

## 3. Repliker och tidslinje

| Tid | Vem | Replik (undertexter ord för ord) |
|---|---|---|
| 0,0–1,5 s | Mannen (vänster) | "I identify as a millionaire." |
| 1,5–3,0 s | Tjejen (höger) | "Can you define millionaire?" |
| 3,0–5,5 s | Mannen | "A millionaire is… rich." |
| 5,5–8,0 s | Tjejen – tittar på hans outfit | "Okay, you're not then." |
| 8,0–10,0 s | Reaktion | Tjejen vänder sig mot kameran och skrattar till (egen bild, inget meme-klipp) |

## 4. Videoprompt (Wan 3.0, start → slut, ca 8 s)

```
Single continuous handheld iPhone shot of a night street interview, slight natural sway, no cuts.
The woman on the right holds the microphone toward the man on the left. 0-1.5s: the man proudly
says "I identify as a millionaire", showing his car key. 1.5-3s: she turns the mic back to herself
and asks "Can you define millionaire?". 3-5.5s: he thinks, then answers "A millionaire is... rich",
nodding confidently. 5.5-8s: she slowly looks him up and down at his oversized shiny suit and fake
chain, raises an eyebrow and says "Okay, you're not then" with a small amused smile; he looks
confused, sunglasses sliding down. Natural lip sync, realistic motion, consistent faces and
icy-blue eyes, no text.
```

## 5. Efterarbete (ffmpeg, gratis)

- Gula, feta, kursiva undertexter ord för ord i mitten av bilden, synkade med replikerna.
- Klipp på 8,0 s till en kort reaktion (2 s): närbild där tjejen skrattar in i kameran.
